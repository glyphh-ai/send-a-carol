## Who this is from

{{name}} is run by Glyphh AI LLC, of Georgetown, Texas ("Glyphh", "we", "us"). This policy says what information {{name}} collects, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It covers {{domain}}.

In these pages a **carol** is our word for the song, whatever the occasion, and for the thing you make. The **card** is the page, with its video, that the person you send a carol to opens.

Questions: [support@glyphh.ai](mailto:support@glyphh.ai).

## Why we use it

- To make your carol: write the words, generate the song, render the video and host the card.
- To deliver it: show the card at its link, email it to the recipients you name, and let the video be downloaded.
- To run your account: sign you in, keep your carol credits right, and deliver what you bought.
- To honour "don't email me again".
- To keep people safe: read reports, remove carols that break the rules, and stop abuse.
- To keep honest books: purchases and the cost ledger.
- To understand and improve the service, using our own counts.
- To meet legal duties and to protect our rights and other people's.

**What we do not do.** We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use your photos to train anything. We do not run face recognition on your photos.

## Where it is stored

- **The database** (accounts, carols' words and details, purchases, recipients, the ledger) is in the United States, hosted by Fly.io.
- **Media** (original photos, resized copies, songs, videos, posters and previews) is kept in Cloudflare R2 object storage, in a private area that only {{name}} can read. The storage is not open to the public.
- **A card's video and poster** are served to viewers through {{domain}}, on the card.

## What is public and what is private

**Anyone who has a card's link can see** the carol as rendered: its video, its poster image and the words that are part of it. No sign-in is needed. The card's name is part of the link, and a short or obvious name can be guessed. See the [Terms](/legal/terms#8-a-card-is-public-to-anyone-with-its-link).

**A recipient you email sees** the email with the card's link.

**Private:** your original photo files, your email address, your recipients' email addresses, what you said to the agent, your purchases and your account. A person who reports a card is not named to the carol's maker.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with the companies that help us run {{name}}, each for its own job:

| Company | What it receives | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fly.io | Everything in the database and on the servers, which Fly.io hosts in the United States. | Hosting. |
| Cloudflare | Your original photos, the resized copies, and each carol's song, video, poster and preview, kept in Cloudflare R2. | Storing media. |
| Stripe | Your email address and what you are buying; the payment itself; your payment details, which you give to Stripe directly. | Payments. |

| ElevenLabs | A music generation service. The words you approved and the style direction for the music. The words contain whatever names and details you put in them. **Your photos and all email addresses are not sent to it.** | Generating the song. |

Each of these companies handles what it receives under its own terms and privacy policy.

We may also share information:

- when you ask us to, such as when you email a card to someone;
- if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety or our rights;
- with a buyer, if the service is sold or reorganised. We will tell you.

## Recipients of a card

If someone sent you a card through {{name}}, we hold your email address because they typed it in. We use it only to send you that card and, if you ask, to make sure we never email you again. We do not add you to any list, send you marketing, or share your address with anyone but the email company that delivers the message.

Every card email has a "don't email me again" link. Use it and we will send no more {{name}} emails to that address, from anyone. To do that we keep the address on our "do not email" list. You can also write to [support@glyphh.ai](mailto:support@glyphh.ai).

## Deleting

**A carol.** Delete a carol in {{name}} and its media is removed from storage. Its card then answers that there is no such page (a "404 not found"). A video that someone has already downloaded or posted elsewhere is a copy we cannot reach.

**Your account.** {{name}} has no delete-my-account button today. To delete your account, write to [support@glyphh.ai](mailto:support@glyphh.ai) from the email address on the account. We will delete your account, your carols and their media. We keep what we must: records of payments, the cost ledger, the "do not email" list and reports, for as long as the law and our safety work require.

**Backups.** Deleted data can remain in backups until they are replaced. [[OWNER: confirm the backup period stated under "How long we keep it"]]

## Your choices and rights

**See and correct.** You can see your carols and your account in {{name}}. For anything else, write to us.

**Emails.** We send a sign-in link only when you ask for one, and a card only when its maker asks. We do not send marketing email today.

**If you are in someone else's card.** See [Photos and people in them](/legal/photos#if-you-are-in-a-card-and-want-it-removed).

**Rights under the law where you live.** Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, and to object to or limit some uses. We will not treat you worse for using a right. To use one, write to [support@glyphh.ai](mailto:support@glyphh.ai); we may need to check that the request comes from you.

## Children

You must be at least 13 to use {{name}}, and 18 for anything that involves money. The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 as a user. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to [support@glyphh.ai](mailto:support@glyphh.ai) and we will delete it.

Cards often show children: a birthday, a new baby, a family at the holidays. A photo of a child, or a child's name, may be put into a carol only by the child's parent or legal guardian, or with that person's permission. See [Photos and people in them](/legal/photos). If you are a parent or guardian and a card shows or names your child without your permission, use the card's report link or write to us and we will take it down.

## Security

We take reasonable care. The site is served over an encrypted connection. Card payments are handled on Stripe's pages, not ours. Photos and other media are kept in private storage, and original files are never given out through a card.

No system is perfectly safe, and we cannot promise that nothing will ever go wrong. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

## People outside the United States

{{name}} is run from the United States, and its servers and database are there. If you use it from another country, your information is sent to and kept in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from yours. The companies named above may process what they receive in other countries too.
