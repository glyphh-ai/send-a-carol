## Pricing

Making a carol costs one carol credit. Credits are sold in packs, in US dollars:

{{packs}}

One-time, not recurring. Nothing renews by itself. We do not keep a card on file to charge later.

Carol credits do not expire.

## All sales are final

{{name}} does not give money back, except where the law gives you a right we cannot exclude. You are told so before you pay, in {{name}} and at checkout. For every pack: One-time purchase. Not refundable.

This covers a pack, credits you did not use, and a carol that was made but that you do not like.

## Why

Each carol costs us money at the moment it is made: the song is generated and the video is rendered by services we pay for each time. That cost cannot be undone.

## When a credit is spent

A credit is spent when the song is generated for a carol, and only then.

- **Free:** writing and rewriting the words with the agent, and previewing, within fair-use daily limits. Before its first purchase, an account may draft {{freeDrafts}} carols with the agent.
- **One credit:** the song. Each paid carol includes {{takes}} takes of the song: the first one and the further takes after it. Use them if a take is not right.
- **Another credit:** a further take of the same carol once its {{takes}} takes are used.

## When something fails

- **A render that fails is tried again.** If rendering the video fails, our system retries it. You do not pay again for a retry.
- **No song, no charge.** If the song could not be generated, no credit is spent.
- **If our system fails to produce a carol after a credit was spent, we restore the credit.** The credit goes back to your account so that you can make the carol again. This is a credit restoration. It is never a money refund.

If a credit was spent, no carol came out and the credit has not come back, write to [support@glyphh.ai](mailto:support@glyphh.ai) with the card's name and we will look at it.

**What is not a failure.** Words and songs are made by AI and can be imperfect: a name sung oddly, a line you do not love, a style that is not quite what you pictured. That is what the takes are for. A carol that was produced, but not to your taste, does not bring a credit back.

## Mistaken payments

If you think a payment was taken by mistake, for example you were charged twice for one purchase, write to [support@glyphh.ai](mailto:support@glyphh.ai) from the email address on your account. Tell us the date and the amount. A person will read it and put right what was our mistake.

Please write to us before you dispute a charge with your card company. It is almost always faster.

## Your rights under the law

Nothing on this page takes away a right that the law where you live gives you and that cannot be given up by agreement. All sales are final except where the law gives you a right we cannot exclude.
