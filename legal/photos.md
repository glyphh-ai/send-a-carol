## What you confirm when you upload a photo

A carol from {{name}} is made from 1 to {{photos}} photos that you upload. (A **carol** is our word for the song, whatever the occasion, and for the thing you make; the **card** is the page, with its video, that the person you send it to opens.) For each photo, you confirm that:

- **you have the right to use it.** You took it, or the person who took it is happy for you to use it this way;
- **the people in it would be fine with it.** They would not mind being in this card, with this song, sent to these people, on a page that anyone with the link can see;
- **it follows the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use).**

If you are not sure about a photo, ask the people in it first, or choose another.

## Photos of children

Children are in a great many cards, for birthdays, new babies and family occasions of every kind, and they are welcome in these.

- If a photo shows a child, **you are that child's parent or legal guardian, or you have the permission of the child's parent or legal guardian** to use it in this carol.
- The same goes for a child's name in the song.
- That covers every child in the photo, not only your own. A photo of your children with their cousins or friends needs the other parents' permission too.
- Nothing about a child in a carol may be anything but wholesome. See the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use#children).
- Remember that a card is public to anyone who has its link. Think about what a stranger could learn about a child from the card, and choose the card's name with care.

## No photos of strangers

Do not upload a photo of someone you do not know, or of someone who does not know you are using it: a person in the street, a photo taken from someone else's social media, a public figure, a neighbour who would be surprised. The same goes for a photo you found online or one a professional photographer took, unless you have the right to use it.

## What is stored, and where

| What | Where it is kept | Who can get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The original file of each photo, as you uploaded it (up to {{photoSize}}) | Privately, in Cloudflare R2 object storage | Only {{name}}, to make your carol. Never a viewer of the card. |
| Resized copies made for the video | Privately, in the same storage | Only {{name}}, to render the video. |
| The finished carol: its video, its poster image and its preview | In the same storage, served through the card at {{domain}} | Anyone who has the card's link. |

An original file may carry what your camera wrote inside it, such as the time and the place the photo was taken. Original files are never given out through a card.

The [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) says more about storage and the companies involved.

## Who can see your photos

- **Anyone who has the card's link sees the finished card:** the video with your photos in it, and its poster image. They never get your original files.
- **Whoever you email the card to** receives the link, and can pass it on.
- **A video can be downloaded.** A copy that someone has saved or posted elsewhere is out of our hands.
- **We look at a carol** only when we need to: when its card is reported, when something has failed, or when you ask support for help.

## What we never do with your photos

- **We do not use your photos to train anything.**
- **We do not run face recognition** on your photos. We do not identify, measure or match the faces in them.
- **We do not send your photos to the AI writing service or to the music generation service.** The words are written from the text you type, and the song is generated from the words.
- We do not use your photos or your carols in advertising, and we do not sell them.

## How to delete

- **A carol.** Delete the carol in {{name}}. Its original photos, the resized copies, the song, the video, the poster and the preview are removed from storage, and its card then answers that there is no such page.
- **Your account.** Write to [support@glyphh.ai](mailto:support@glyphh.ai) from the email address on the account, and we will delete the account, its carols and their media.
- **Backups.** Deleted files can remain in backups for a time. [[OWNER: how long storage backups are kept, and so how long deleted photos can remain in them]]

## If you are in a card and want it removed

You do not have to be the one who made a carol to ask for its card to come down.

- Use the **"report this card"** link on the card and say that you are in it, or that your child is; or
- write to [support@glyphh.ai](mailto:support@glyphh.ai) with the card's link.

You do not need to give a reason beyond that. A person reads each request. We may ask a question to be sure which card and which person you mean, and we may hide the card while we look. When someone pictured in a card, or the parent or guardian of a child pictured in it, asks for it to be removed, we take it down. The maker is not told who asked.

Taking a card down does not bring back the credit that made it. See the [Refund Policy](/legal/refunds).
