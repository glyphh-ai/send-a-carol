## What a carol is for

{{name}} makes singing cards for any occasion: warm, funny or heartfelt greetings for people you know. These rules keep it that way. They are part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms), and they apply to everything you put into a carol: the photos, the names and details, what you ask the agent to write, the name of the card's page, and who you send it to.

A **carol** is our word for the song, whatever the occasion, and for the thing you make. The **card** is the page, with its video, that the person you send it to opens.

## People

Do not make, ask for or send a carol that:

- is meant to **demean, harass, bully, humiliate or threaten** a person, or to stir others up against them. Gentle teasing between people who love each other is what a good birthday card is made of; a carol made to hurt is not;
- contains **sexual content** of any kind, in the words or in the photos;
- contains **hate**: attacks on people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or anything like it;
- promotes violence or self-harm, or anything unlawful;
- reveals someone's private information, such as a home address or a phone number, that they would not want shared.

## Children

Children are welcome in a carol, in the way they are in a family's own cards.

- **Nothing about a child that is not wholesome.** No sexual, violent, frightening, mocking or demeaning content about a child, in the words or in the photos. This rule has no exceptions, and we may report what the law requires us to report.
- A child's photo or name goes into a carol only from the child's parent or legal guardian, or with that person's permission. See [Photos and people in them](/legal/photos).
- Do not put a child's full name together with details such as a school or a home address. A card is public to anyone who has its link.

## Honesty

- **Do not impersonate to deceive.** Do not make a carol that pretends to come from another person, a company, a charity or a public figure in order to mislead. A birthday carol signed "from the dog" is fine. One that passes itself off as from a real person or business that did not send it is not.
- Do not use a carol to trick people out of money or information, or to spread claims you know to be false about a real person.

## Songs and other people's work

The song in your carol is new, written and sung for it. Keep it that way.

- **Do not copy a copyrighted song.** Do not ask for, paste in or upload the words or the melody of a song that is under copyright, in whole or in part.
- **No artist names and no song titles in what you ask for.** Do not ask for a song "in the style of" a named singer or band, "like" a named song, or in a named person's voice. Describe the feeling instead: slow and warm, bright and jazzy, a children's choir, a country waltz.
- **Old songs may inspire.** Traditional songs and tunes that are in the public domain may inspire the mood and the shape of your song.
- Do not put other people's logos, artwork or characters into a carol unless you have the right to.

We may refuse a request, or change it, where it appears to name an artist or a copyrighted song.

## Photos

Use only photos you have the right to use, of people who would be fine with it. No photos of strangers. No photos taken from someone else's social media, a photographer's site or the news without permission. [Photos and people in them](/legal/photos) says this in full.

## No spam

- **Email a card only to people you know**, who would expect to hear from you: family, friends, colleagues, your own customers who know you.
- **Never a marketing list.** Do not send to a bought, rented, scraped or collected list of addresses.
- Do not try to get round the limit of {{recipients}} recipients for each carol by making copies of a carol to reach more people with the same message.
- Do not try to get round a recipient's "don't email me again" choice.

## The service itself

Do not:

- scrape or collect cards, pages or other people's information from {{name}}, or guess at card names in bulk to find other people's cards;
- use bots, scripts or other automated tools to make accounts or carols, or to overload the service;
- get round limits, payments, bans or security;
- probe or attack the service, or try to get at someone else's account, photos or carols;
- take the songs out of carols to build a music library, or use them where the [Terms](/legal/terms#7-your-carol-and-what-you-may-do-with-it) do not allow;
- resell {{name}} or run it for other people as a service of your own, unless we have agreed to it in writing;
- use {{name}} to break the law.

## Reporting a card

Every card has a **"report this card"** link. Anyone can use it, signed in or not: the person who received the card, a person who appears in it, a parent, or a stranger who came across it. You can also write to [support@glyphh.ai](mailto:support@glyphh.ai) with the card's link.

**What we do.** A person reads each report. We look at the card, and we may hide it while we do. If it breaks these rules we remove it. We do not tell the carol's maker who reported it. If you tell us how to reach you, we may write back to say what we did.

**If you are in a card** and want it gone, you do not have to show that it breaks a rule. Tell us, and see [Photos and people in them](/legal/photos#if-you-are-in-a-card-and-want-it-removed).

## What happens when a rule is broken

Depending on how serious it is and whether it has happened before, we may:

- refuse to make, render or send a carol;
- hide or remove a carol;
- stop an account from sending email;
- stop an account from making new carols;
- close the account;
- tell the authorities, where the law requires it or someone is in danger.

All sales are final, as the [Refund Policy](/legal/refunds) says. A carol removed for breaking these rules, or an account closed for it, does not bring back a credit or any money, except where the law gives you a right we cannot exclude.

If you think we got it wrong, write to [support@glyphh.ai](mailto:support@glyphh.ai). A person will read it.
