How it works

Three little steps, one very big smile.

We call the song a carol, whatever the occasion: a birthday in July, a thank you in March, a love song on a Tuesday.

Say who it is for

Chat with the agent: their name, what they mean to you, a few true details (the dog, the inside joke, this year's big moment). Add photos if you like.

It is written, then sung

The agent writes the carol in a couple of minutes. Change any line you want, then have it sung and set to a video with your photos dancing along.

Send the link or the video

Your card gets its own page at {{domain}}/your-name. Text the link, post the video, or email it straight from here.

Questions

Good to know before you sing.

How long does it take?

A couple of minutes to write it with the agent, then a few minutes for the song to be sung and the video to be made. You can close the page while it works: it will be waiting for you.

What do I get?

A link like {{domain}}/your-name that plays your card for anyone you send it to. A video file in two shapes (tall for stories and reels, square for feeds and email). And a poster image for previews.

How do I share it?

Any way you like. Copy the link into a text or a chat, download the video and post it, or email it from here to the people you choose.

What happens to my photos?

They are stored privately and used only to make your card. Delete a photo, or the whole carol, any time and they are removed. More about photos.

Can I write my own words?

Yes. Give the agent your own lyrics or one line that must be in the song, and edit every line of what it writes before it is sung.

Is there anything it will not write?

It writes kind things. It will not write anything unkind or sexual about someone, and it will not copy a copyrighted song: every carol is an original.

Can I get my money back?

{{finalsale}} Writing and previewing are free, so you can be sure you love it before you buy. Read the policy.

Is it only for holidays?

Not at all. A carol is our word for the song, for any occasion: birthdays, thank yous, love, congratulations, the holidays, or no reason at all.
